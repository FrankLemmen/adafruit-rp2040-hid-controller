Setting the key colors

Every key can have its own color. You set them with the knob alone: no software and no files needed. The colors are remembered after you unplug the pad.

Four steps

1Push the knob to start

Press the knob (top right) once. The pad enters programming mode, and key 1 (top left) starts blinking white.

While you program, the keys don't send presses to your PC or game.

2Turn left to pick a color

Each click to the left shows the next color on the blinking key:

After White it starts again at Off. "Off" blinks dim white so you can still see which key you're on.

3Turn right to confirm

One click to the right saves the color on the current key, and the next key starts blinking white.

Keys go in reading order. After key 12 it returns to key 1, so you can keep going around as long as you like:

To skip a key, just turn right without turning left first. It keeps its color.

4Push the knob to finish

Press the knob again. Programming mode ends, all keys show their new colors, and the keys work as normal gamepad buttons again.

The colors are saved inside the pad and come back the next time you plug it in.

Good to know

Only "right" savesA color you picked with left turns but didn't confirm with a right turn is dropped when you push the knob to exit.
Knob is quiet otherwiseOutside programming mode, turning the knob does nothing.
Turning a key offChoose "Off" (the first color) and confirm with a right turn.
Brightness and speedChange BRIGHTNESS or BLINK_SECONDS at the top of code.py.
